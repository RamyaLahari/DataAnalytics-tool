Partition Trace

    Pseudocode

    quicksort(low, high)
  if low < high
    pivotIndex = partition(low, high)
    quicksort(low, pivotIndex - 1)
    quicksort(pivotIndex + 1, high)

partition(low, high)
  choose pivot
  scan items from low to high - 1
  move smaller items left of pivot
  place pivot in its final position

    How To Read It

    Blue marks the pivot, gold marks values being compared, green marks sorted positions, and red flashes when values swap.
